2. How to Submit an Official Appeal

If your account has been flagged for "User Error: Please login with Google" or a regional restriction, you must submit a formal appeal.

  1. 1

    Go to the Official Login Portal and attempt to authenticate using your primary email address.

  2. 2

    Screenshot any error codes (e.g., ERR_AUTH_GOOGLE) provided during the session.

  3. 3

    Send these details along with your public wallet address to our 2026 High-Priority desk via the dashboard.

Frequently Asked Questions

My account says "User Error" - what do I do?

This usually means your session has expired or you attempted to connect via an unsupported third-party browser. Return to the home screen and click "Sign In" to refresh your session.

Can I appeal a trade outcome?

Outcome appeals must be submitted within 2 hours of market resolution. After this window, the smart contract settles permanently on the blockchain.
