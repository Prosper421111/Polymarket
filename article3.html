How to request moneyback or withdrawal on polymarket to your bank

By Finance Compliance Team

Last Modified: Feb 26, 2026

Withdrawing your earnings from Polymarket to a traditional bank account has been significantly streamlined for the 2026 fiscal year. Whether you are looking for a standard withdrawal of profits or a full "moneyback" refund of your initial deposit, the process is now handled through our secure Fiat Gateway.

01 Check Your Tradable Balance

Before initiating a bank transfer, ensure all active market positions are closed. "Moneyback" requests can only be processed on unsettled USDC or Polygon-native assets. If you currently have active "Yes/No" shares, you must sell them back to the order book to convert them into withdrawable cash.

Supported Bank Methods

  • Instant SEPA (Europe)
  • ACH Transfer (USA/Canada)
  • SWIFT Wire (International)

02 Linking Your Bank Account

Navigate to your dashboard and select "Cash Out." In 2026, Polymarket utilizes an encrypted API to link directly to your banking institution. To maintain the highest level of security, the name on your bank account must match the legal name verified during your Google Sign-In process.

03 The Moneyback Verification

If you are requesting a refund due to a technical error or an account restriction (Moneyback), you must provide a transaction hash from your history. Once the request is submitted, our compliance team reviews the blockchain logs to authorize the release of funds to your linked IBAN or Routing Number.

Withdrawal Timelines

Standard withdrawals typically reach your bank within 1-2 business days. High-volume "Moneyback" requests may require an additional 24-hour verification period to prevent fraudulent wash-trading activities.

Final Authorization

To complete your request, you must log in to the portal and sign the withdrawal certificate. Failure to sign this certificate within 30 minutes of the request will result in an automated cancellation for your protection.

Withdraw Winnings

Access the secure portal to link your bank and request your moneyback instantly.

Official Financial Guide
